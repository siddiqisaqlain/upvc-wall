Materials & finishes

what it is made of decides how long it lasts

Stainless steel: 304 vs 202

SS 304 — ~18–20% chromium, 8–10.5% nickel. Bathrooms, kitchens, outdoors.

SS 202 — only 4–6% nickel, 7.5–10% manganese instead. Cheaper and looks identical — but rusts near water, sea air and cleaning chemicals.

SS 316 (with molybdenum) — near the sea.

Magnet test?useless — 304 AND 202 are both non-magnetic. Ask for the grade stamp or a test certificate

The other metals

Brass — heavy, resists corrosion; lacquer dulls near salt air.

Zinc alloy (zamak) — die-cast and always plated; corrodes once plating wears. The brass version costs ~30–80% more.

Aluminium — light, extruded: profile handles.

Mixed metals (SS hinge on a plain-steel frame) corrode each other.

Finishes seen in the market

same handle, different coat

Check the box

Grade (304 / 202), size, finish, brand, ISI mark. Feel the weight; look at the back face.

Hinges

the part that carries the whole door

Hinge family

elevation, door edge on the right · Indian Standard in brackets

How many per door?

Up to 1524 mm high → 2. Up to 2286 mm → 3. Then +1 per extra 762 mm.

So a normal 2100 mm Indian door takes 3 hinges. Heavy or busy doors → ball-bearing hinges.

Cabinet hinges: ask for the crank

35 mm cup, ~110° opening, adjustable after fitting, optional soft-close.

0 crank = full overlay (door covers the side) · 8 crank = half overlay (two doors share one side) · 16 crank = inset (door sits inside).

8 crank or 16 crank?ask before the carpenter drills the 35 mm cup

Bolts & aldrops

simple sliding steel that keeps a door shut

Know these

Tower bolt — surface bolt on the inside face of doors & windows (IS 204; SS: IS 15833).

Aldrop ("kunda") — heavy sliding bolt that takes a padlock, so the door locks from outside (IS 2681 brass · IS 281 MS · IS 15834 SS).

Flush bolt — recessed in the edge of the second leaf of a double door (IS 5187).

Gate hook — hook + eye that holds a shutter open.

04 / 09

Locks & levers

what turns, what latches, what locks

Mortise lock, opened up

door edge on the right · lever-type mortice locks: IS 16015

Lock types

Mortise — body sunk into the door edge; handles + cylinder on the faces.

Cylindrical / tubular — bored through the door; privacy button for toilets (IS 16016).

Rim lock / night latch — screwed on the inside face of the main door (IS 1019).

Order by backset, centre distance, door thickness and handing.

Digital locks

PIN, fingerprint, RFID card or app — plus a mechanical key. Godrej Catus Touch Plus: fits 35–65 mm doors; flat battery? power it from a power bank outside.

Lever or knob?levers are easier for elderly, wet or full hands — accessibility guidelines prefer them
05 / 09

Door pull handles

the handshake of the building

Choosing a pull

Sold by hole centres: 300, 450, 600, 900, 1000, 1200 mm; overall length ≈ centres + 100 mm.

Back-to-back pairs bolt through the door — one handle each side; suits timber or 8–12 mm toughened glass.

A pull does not hold the door shut — it needs a latch, lock or closer.

Push side or pull side?a flat push plate on one face tells people which way
06 / 09

Cabinet handles & knobs

the biggest wall in every hardware shop

Sized by hole centres

Pulls are sold by the centre-to-centre (C/C) distance of the screws: 96, 128, 160, 192, 224 mm…

Replacing one? Measure C/C first, not the overall length.

Handle-less kitchens

Gola profiles — aluminium G / J / C channels along the shutter top give a finger grip (Häfele "Profin Gola", Ebco). Or push-to-open.

07 / 09

Furniture fittings

what makes a modular kitchen move

Drawers

Telescopic ball-bearing channels — side-fixed; e.g. Ebco: 35 / 45 kg, 250–500 mm long.

Under-mount runners — hidden below the drawer, soft-close (40 / 70 kg classes).

Tandem box — metal-sided drawer system, up to ~65 kg.

Choose by load and length = carcass depth.

Overhead shutters: lift-ups

Bi-fold (folds in the middle) · swing-up (lifts up and over) · parallel lift — with built-in damping, sized by the flap's weight.

Made in India tooHettich's Indore plant makes ~60 million hinges a year
